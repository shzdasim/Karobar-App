# Training Lesson 6: Pharmacy & Medical Store Owners

**Objectives:** Monitor store health in 60 seconds via Dashboard, prevent expiry/theft loss, control supervisor overrides with Operator PINs, and review Net Profit & Narcotics compliance.

## Instructions & Examples
1. **Daily 60-Second Check:** Open **Dashboard** (`/dashboard`) → review **Net Sales**, **Gross Margin**, **Action Center** (Expiring Soon, Low Stock, Pending Demands), and **Financials & Credit**.
2. **Loss Prevention:** Require daily **Cash Drawer** opening/closing (`/cash-drawer`), keep **Blind Count Mode** ON for Stocktakes (`/stocktake-sessions`), and restrict deletion/discount permissions in **Roles** (`/roles`).
3. **Compliance & Profit:** Review **Net Profit (P&L)** (`/reports/net-profit`), **Near Expiry** (`/reports/near-expiry-product`), and **Narcotics Register** (`/reports/narcotics`) monthly.

## Practice Exercises
1. Switch **Dashboard** to *This Month*, open **Operations & Stock**, and filter **Near Expiry Batch Radar** for `3 Months`.
2. Review **Cash Drawer** past shifts to confirm zero unexplained cash variances.
