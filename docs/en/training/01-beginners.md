# Training Lesson 1: Beginners (First-Time Store Staff)

**Objectives:** Log in safely, navigate the sidebar, look up medicines by name/salt/rack, and record out-of-stock requests.

## Instructions & Examples
1. **Login & Sidebar:** Sign in on `/` with your personal email and password. Star your favorite screens (e.g., *Barcode POS*, *User Demands*) so they stay pinned at the top.
2. **Finding Medicines:** Open **Products** (`/products`) and search by brand name (*Panadol*), generic salt (*Paracetamol*), or rack (`R-01`).
3. **Shortbook:** When a medicine is out of stock, open **User Demands → New Product Demand** (`/user-demands/create`) and log the medicine name and patient's phone number.

## Practice Exercises
1. Log in, star **Barcode POS** and **User Demands**, and search for 3 medicines by generic salt.
2. Record a sample demand for *Insulatard Penfill* (`Qty: 2`) with a phone number.
