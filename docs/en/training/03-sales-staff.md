# Training Lesson 3: Sales Employees & Wholesale Staff

**Objectives:** Create Retail & Wholesale credit invoices, apply Trade Schemes, use customer wholesale price lists, and manage regular refill customers.

## Instructions & Examples
1. **Credit & Wholesale Billing:** Open **Wholesale Invoices → Create** (`/wholesale-invoices/create`), pick a regular clinic (*Al-Shifa Clinic*), enter **Pack Qty** (e.g., `10` packs), and verify Trade Scheme **Bonus** auto-fills.
2. **Credit Limits:** If a credit limit warning appears, collect a partial payment or request Manager **Credit Override**.
3. **Regular Refill Customers:** In **Customers** (`/customers`), filter **Due Customers** and click **Shift All Regular Products** to build a patient's monthly refill bill in one click.

## Practice Exercises
1. Create a Credit Wholesale Invoice for `10` packs with a partial payment of `Rs. 2,000` and verify the remaining balance appears on **Customer Ledger**.
2. Open **Customers**, view a regular customer's refill items, and generate their refill invoice.
