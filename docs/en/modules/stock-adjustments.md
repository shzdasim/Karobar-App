# Stock Adjustments — User Manual (English)

> **Who uses this screen?** Store Managers, Inventory Controllers, and Pharmacy Owners.  
> **Where to find it:** Sidebar → **Stock & Inventory → Stock Adjustments** (`/stock-adjustments`) or **Add Stock Adjustment** (`/stock-adjustments/create`).

---

## A. Purpose and Overview

The **Stock Adjustments** module is the official way to correct verified stock differences—such as broken bottles, spilled syrups, found extra boxes, or opening count corrections—while keeping a permanent audit trail of who changed stock and why.

---

## B. What Users Can Do

- **Search Adjustments** (`/stock-adjustments`) by Posted Number or Note.
- **Create Positive (+) or Negative (-) Adjustments** (`/stock-adjustments/create`) at the exact **Batch** level with mandatory reason notes.
- **View Worth Adjusted** (total financial impact of the adjustment).
- **View or Print** individual adjustment vouchers (`/stock-adjustments/:id`). *(Adjustments created automatically by a finalized Stocktake cannot be deleted directly.)*

## D. Every Field, Button, Tab and Option

| Element | Type | What You Enter / What Happens |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Posted Number / Date** | Auto / Date | Auto-assigned voucher number and date of adjustment. |
| **Note (Reason) \*** | Text (Required) | Mandatory explanation (e.g., *"2 bottles broken during shelf cleaning"*). |
| **Batch # & Expiry** | Dropdown / Display | Exact batch whose stock is being increased or decreased. |
| **Adjustment Qty** | Number | Positive (`+`) to add stock, negative (`-`) to deduct stock. |
| **Total Worth Adjusted** | Read-Only | Financial value of the stock added or written off. |

---

## E. Step-by-Step Instructions

1. Go to **Stock Adjustments → Add Stock Adjustment** (`/stock-adjustments/create`).
2. Enter a clear **Note (Reason)** (e.g., *"Broken 1 bottle of Augmentin Duo Syrup on shelf"*).
3. Select the medicine and the exact **Batch #**.
4. Enter `-1` (negative) to deduct 1 unit, or `+2` (positive) if 2 extra units were found.
5. Check **Total Worth Adjusted** and click **Save**.

---

## F. Practical Business Examples & Simple Calculations

### Example: Broken Syrup Write-Off
1 bottle of *Augmentin Duo Syrup* (Unit Purchase Cost `Rs. 320`) breaks on the counter:
- **Adjustment Qty** = `-1`
- **Total Worth Adjusted** = `-1 × Rs. 320 = -Rs. 320.00`
- **Stock Balance:** If batch had `12` units, new stock becomes `12 - 1 = 11` units.

## G. Expected Results

## H. Common Mistakes and Solutions

| Mistake | Why It Happens | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cannot delete a Stock Adjustment** | It was auto-generated by finalizing a **Stocktake Session**. | Stocktake adjustments are locked for audit integrity. Create a new corrective adjustment if needed. |
| **Save blocked for missing Note** | **Note (Reason)** is required for every adjustment. | Enter a clear reason explaining why stock is being changed. |
