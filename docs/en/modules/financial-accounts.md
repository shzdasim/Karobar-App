# Financial Accounts (Cash, Bank & Wallets) — User Manual (English)

> **Who uses this screen?** Pharmacy Owners, Accountants, and Store Managers.  
> **Where to find it:** Sidebar → **Finance & Ledgers → Financial Accounts** (`/accounts`).

---

## A. Purpose and Overview

The **Financial Accounts** module tracks every place your pharmacy holds money—**Cash in Hand**, **Bank Accounts** (Meezan, HBL, UBL, etc.), and **Mobile Wallets** (JazzCash, EasyPaisa).

---

## B. What Users Can Do

- **Create & Manage Accounts:** Add Cash, Bank, Card POS, or Mobile Wallet accounts with opening balances.
- **Transfer Between Accounts:** Record cash deposits from the counter drawer into your Bank account (or wallet-to-bank transfers).
- **View Account Transaction Ledger:** Inspect every deposit, withdrawal, sale receipt, supplier payment, and expense per account.

---

## C. Screen-by-Screen Explanation

![Financial Accounts](../../../Screenshots/Financial-Accounts.webp)

---

## D. Every Field, Button, Tab and Option

| Element | Type | What It Does |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Add Account** | Button | Creates a new Cash, Bank, or Mobile Wallet account with an opening balance. |
| **Transfer Funds** | Button | Moves money from one account to another (e.g., *Cash in Hand → HBL Business Account*). |
| **Account Filter** | Selector | Filters the transaction history to one specific account. |

---

## E. Step-by-Step Instructions

### Depositing Counter Cash into Your Bank Account
1. Go to **Finance & Ledgers → Financial Accounts** (`/accounts`).
2. Click **Transfer Funds**.
3. Set **From Account** = *Cash in Hand*, **To Account** = *Meezan Bank*.
4. Enter **Amount** (e.g., `Rs. 50,000`) and Note (*"Daily bank deposit"*).
5. Click **Save**.

---

- After Deposit: *Cash in Hand* = `Rs. 15,000` | *Meezan Bank* = `Rs. 170,000` (Total store liquidity unchanged at `Rs. 185,000`).

- Account balances update immediately; inter-account transfers appear in both source and destination histories.

## H. Common Mistakes and Solutions

| Mistake | Why It Happens | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **JazzCash/Card payment went into Cash balance** | Cashier left the payment account on *Cash* instead of *JazzCash/Bank*. | Check the account selected at POS/Payment Voucher and transfer or edit if needed. |
