# Operating Expenses — User Manual (English)

> **Who uses this screen?** Pharmacy Owners, Managers, and Accountants.  
> **Where to find it:** Sidebar → **Finance & Ledgers → Operating Expenses** (`/expenses`).

---

## A. Purpose and Overview

The **Operating Expenses** module records all non-inventory shop costs—such as **Shop Rent**, **Electricity / WAPDA Bill**, **Staff Salaries**, **Internet**, **Packaging Bags**, and **Daily Tea/Refreshments**—so your **Net Profit (P&L)** report shows true profit.

---

## B. What Users Can Do

- **Manage Expense Categories** (via **Categories** modal).
- **Record Daily Expenses** paid from **Cash Drawer** or any **Bank / Mobile Wallet** account, with optional receipt attachment.
- **Set Up Recurring Expenses** (e.g., Monthly Shop Rent) so Karobar-App logs them automatically on schedule.
- **Filter, Edit, or Delete** expenses by category, account, and date range.

## D. Every Field, Button, Tab and Option

| Element | Type | What You Enter / What Happens |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Add Expense** | Button | Opens form to record a new expense. |
| **Categories** | Modal Button | Add or rename expense categories (*Rent*, *Utilities*, *Maintenance*, etc.). |
| **Paid From (Account)** | Dropdown | Deducts the expense from **Cash** (active Cash Drawer shift) or **Bank / Wallet**. |
| **Recurring Toggle** | Switch | Schedules monthly/periodic automatic expense posting. |

---

## E. Step-by-Step Instructions

1. Go to **Finance & Ledgers → Operating Expenses** (`/expenses`).
2. Click **Add Expense**.
3. Choose **Category** (*Electricity / Utilities*), enter **Amount** (`Rs. 18,500`), select **Account** (*Meezan Bank* or *Cash*), and add **Note** (*"WAPDA Bill Nov"*).
4. Click **Save**.

---

## F. Practical Business Examples & Simple Calculations

### Example: Impact on Monthly Net Profit
- **Monthly Gross Profit** (Sales minus Cost of Medicines) = `Rs. 240,000`
- **Monthly Operating Expenses:** Rent (`Rs. 45,000`) + Electricity (`Rs. 18,500`) + Staff Salaries (`Rs. 60,000`) + Tea/Misc (`Rs. 4,500`) = `Rs. 128,000`
- **True Net Profit** = `Rs. 240,000 - Rs. 128,000 = Rs. 112,000`

---

## G. Expected Results

- The selected Financial Account (and active Cash Drawer if Cash) decreases immediately, and **Net Profit (P&L)** updates.

---

## H. Common Mistakes and Solutions

| Mistake | Why It Happens | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cash Drawer shows shortage at night** | Staff paid for tea or courier from the drawer without logging it here. | Add the petty cash expense under **Expenses** using the **Cash** account. |
