# Categories & Brands — User Manual (English)

> **Who uses this screen?** Inventory Managers, Data-Entry Staff, and Pharmacy Owners.  
> **Where to find it:** Sidebar → **Items & Masters → Categories** (`/categories`) and **Brands** (`/brands`).

---

## A. Purpose and Overview

**Categories** (*Tablets*, *Syrups*, *Injections*, *Surgical*, *Cosmetics*) and **Brands** (*GSK*, *Abbott*, *Getz Pharma*, *Sami*) keep your medicine catalog neatly organized for fast search, smart reorder forecasting, and brand-wise sales reporting.

---

## B. What Users Can Do

- **Add, Edit, Search, and Delete** Categories (`/categories`) and Brands (`/brands`).
- **Bulk Import / Export CSV:** Upload distributor brand or category lists in seconds, or export existing lists to Excel/CSV.

| Element | Type | What It Does |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Add / Edit** | Form / Modal | Enter Category or Brand name and save. |
| **Import** | Button | Opens CSV bulk import modal. |
| **Export** | Button | Downloads all categories or brands as a CSV file. |

---

## E. Step-by-Step Instructions

1. Go to **Categories** (`/categories`) or **Brands** (`/brands`).
2. Click **Add**, type the name (e.g., *Antibiotics* or *Getz Pharma*), and click **Save**.
3. To bulk-load many items at once, click **Import**, download the sample CSV template, fill in names, and upload.

## F. Practical Business Examples

### Example: Brand-Wise Reordering

## H. Common Mistakes and Solutions

| Mistake | Why It Happens | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cannot delete a Brand or Category** | Active products are still linked to it. | Reassign those products to another Brand/Category first. |
