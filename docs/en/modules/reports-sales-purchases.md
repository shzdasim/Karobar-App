# Sales & Purchase Reports — User Manual (English)

> **Who uses this screen?** Pharmacy Owners, Managers, Purchase Staff, and Accountants.  
> **Where to find it:** Sidebar → **Reports → Sales, Purchases & Returns** (`/reports/sale-detail`, `/reports/purchase-detail`, `/reports/purchase-summary`, `/reports/sale-returns`, `/reports/purchase-returns`).

---

## A. Purpose and Overview

These reports give you complete item-by-item and invoice-by-invoice visibility into what medicines were sold, what was purchased from distributors, and what was returned over any date range.

---

## B. What Users Can Do

- **Sale Detail (`/reports/sale-detail`):** Inspect every sold item, quantity, bonus, discount, tax, and line total; filter by customer, product, or date; export CSV/PDF or print.
- **Purchase Detail (`/reports/purchase-detail`):** Inspect every purchased item, batch, bonus, trade discount, and supplier; export CSV/PDF or print.
- **Purchase Summary (`/reports/purchase-summary`):** Supplier-wise and date-wise purchase totals.
- **Sale Returns & Purchase Returns Reports (`/reports/sale-returns`, `/reports/purchase-returns`):** Audit all returned goods and refund/credit amounts.

## D. Every Field, Button, Tab and Option

| Element | Type | What It Does |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Date Range & Party/Product Filters** | Filters | Narrows the report to specific days, suppliers, customers, or medicines. |
| **Export CSV / PDF / Print** | Buttons | Exports data for Excel, saves a formatted PDF, or prints directly. |

---

## E. Step-by-Step Instructions

1. Open **Reports → Sale Detail** (`/reports/sale-detail`) or **Purchase Detail** (`/reports/purchase-detail`).
2. Pick **From Date** and **To Date** (and optional Customer/Supplier).
3. Review the itemized rows and totals, then click **Export PDF** or **Print**.

---

## F. Practical Business Examples

### Example: Auditing Distributor Discounts
Filter **Purchase Detail** for *Muller & Phipps* for the last 30 days to verify that every agreed trade discount (`10%`) and bonus pack was properly entered on your bills.

---

## G. Expected Results

- Filtered totals match your Sale Invoices, Purchase Invoices, and Return registers for the same period.

---

## H. Common Mistakes and Solutions

| Mistake | Why It Happens | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Report looks empty** | Date range is set to Today only, or a party filter is still selected. | Widen **From / To Date** and clear extra filters. |
