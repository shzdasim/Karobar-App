# Store, Theme & License Settings — User Manual (English)

> **Who uses this screen?** Pharmacy Owners and System Administrators.  
> **Where to find it:** Sidebar → **System & Settings → Settings** (`/settings`) and License Activation (`/activate`).

---

## A. Purpose and Overview

The **Settings** center is where you configure your pharmacy's identity, currency, sale mode, clinical rules, supervisor PINs, appearance/theme, FBR tax, printers, document sharing, LAN network, Cube AI, and software license.

---

## B. What Users Can Do (All Settings Tabs)

- **Store Profile:** Store name, address, phone, Drug License #, and logo.
- **Sales & Currency:** Currency symbol/code (`PKR / Rs.`) and Retail vs. Retail + Wholesale mode.
- **Pharmacy & Stickers:** Default expiry warning windows and clinical/sticker options.
- **Operator 4-Digit PIN:** Manage supervisor PINs for POS overrides.
- **FBR Tax & Fiscalization** *(License: `fbr`)*: Configure Pakistan FBR POS ID and tax integration.
- **Appearance & Theme:** Light/Dark mode and color palette customization.
- **Document Sharing & Print Layouts:** WhatsApp, SMTP Email, PDF Design, and Thermal/A4 Printer setup.
- **Server & Terminals / Cube AI / Software License:** LAN pairing, AI assistant keys, and license activation.

## D. Every Field, Button, Tab and Option

| Tab | Key Settings |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Store Profile** | Business Name, Address, Phone, Logo, Drug License. |
| **Sales & Currency** | Currency (`Rs.` / `PKR`), Wholesale toggle, Max cashier discount %. |
| **Operator 4-Digit PIN** | Enables/manages 4-digit supervisor PINs. |
| **Appearance & Theme** | Light/Dark mode and accent color theme. |
| **Software License** | Displays active license tier, enabled modules, machine binding, and key activation. |

---

## E. Step-by-Step Instructions

1. Open **System & Settings → Settings** (`/settings`).
2. Click the tab you want to configure (e.g., **Store Profile** or **Appearance & Theme**).
3. Update the values and click **Save**.

---

## F. Practical Business Examples

### Example: Updating Store Header on Receipts
Update your **Phone Number** and **Drug License #** in **Settings → Store Profile** and save—every new thermal and A4 receipt immediately prints the updated header.

---

## G. Expected Results

- Saved settings apply across all screens (and across connected LAN terminals after sync).

---

## H. Common Mistakes and Solutions

| Mistake | Why It Happens | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A Settings tab or module is locked/hidden** | Your current license tier does not include that add-on module. | Open **Settings → Software License** to check active modules. |
