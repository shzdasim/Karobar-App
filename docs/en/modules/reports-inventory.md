# Inventory, Expiry & Narcotics Reports — User Manual (English)

> **Who uses this screen?** Inventory Managers, Pharmacists, Regulatory Auditors, and Pharmacy Owners.  
> **Where to find it:** Sidebar → **Reports → Inventory & Stock** (`/reports/current-stock`, `/reports/reorder`, `/reports/product-comprehensive`, `/reports/near-expiry-product`, `/reports/stock-adjustment`, `/reports/narcotics`).

---

## A. Purpose and Overview

These reports give you complete visibility into your pharmacy's shelf stock, total inventory valuation, low-stock reorder needs, upcoming expiry risks, stock adjustment history, and **Narcotics & Controlled Drugs** regulatory registers.

---

## B. What Users Can Do

- **Current Stock (`/reports/current-stock`):** View live on-hand quantities and stock valuation across all medicines, filter by category/brand/supplier, and export CSV/PDF or Print.
- **Low Stock & Reorder (`/reports/reorder`):** List items below minimum stock threshold.
- **Product Comprehensive (`/reports/product-comprehensive`):** Full history of a product (opening, purchased, sold, returned, adjusted, remaining).
- **Near Expiry Product (`/reports/near-expiry-product`):** Printable/exportable list of batches expiring within your chosen window.
- **Stock Adjustment Report (`/reports/stock-adjustment`):** Audit all manual and stocktake adjustments.
- **Narcotics & Controlled Drugs (`/reports/narcotics`):** Regulatory register tracking controlled/narcotic stock and sales.

## D. Every Field, Button, Tab and Option

| Report | Key Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Current Stock** | Live shelf quantity and total purchase/retail value of your store. |
| **Low Stock & Reorder** | Medicines running low that need ordering. |
| **Product Comprehensive** | Complete stock-in vs. stock-out movement summary per product. |
| **Near Expiry Product** | Batches approaching expiry for distributor returns. |
| **Stock Adjustment Report** | Audit log of all stock write-offs and corrections. |
| **Narcotics & Controlled Drugs** | Compliance register for controlled/narcotic medicines. |

---

## E. Step-by-Step Instructions

1. Open any report under **Reports → Inventory & Stock**.
2. Set your filters (Date range, Category, Brand, Supplier, or Expiry days).
3. Click **Export CSV**, **Export PDF**, or **Print**.

---

## F. Practical Business Examples

### Example: Monthly Regulatory Narcotics Check
Open **Reports → Narcotics & Controlled Drugs** (`/reports/narcotics`) at month-end, filter the month's dates, and print the register to keep alongside your physical DRAP/inspector file.

---

## G. Expected Results

- Live inventory valuation reflects current batch stock; Narcotics register matches all controlled drug sales and disposals.

---

## H. Common Mistakes and Solutions

| Mistake | Why It Happens | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Controlled medicine missing from Narcotics Report** | Product was not flagged as *Narcotic / Controlled* in **Edit Product**. | Edit the product (`/products`) and set **Narcotic / Controlled** or **Drug Schedule = Controlled / Narcotic**. |
