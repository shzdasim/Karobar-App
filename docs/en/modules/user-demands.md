# User Demands (Shortbook) — User Manual (English)

> **Who uses this screen?** Counter Cashiers, Salesmen, and Purchase Managers.  
> **Where to find it:** Sidebar → **Stock & Inventory → User Demands** (`/user-demands`) or **New Product Demand** (`/user-demands/create`).

---

## A. Purpose and Overview

The **User Demands (Shortbook)** module replaces the paper "shortage notebook" at your counter. Whenever a patient asks for a medicine that is out of stock or not yet in your catalog, staff log it here in 5 seconds so your purchase manager remembers to order it.

---

## B. What Users Can Do

- **Log a Demand** (`/user-demands/create`) for an existing product or a brand-new requested product name, with optional customer contact and note.
- **Filter Demands by Status:** *Pending*, *Ordered*, *Fulfilled*, *Cancelled*, or *All*.
- **Update Demand Status** directly from the table (*Ordered*, *Fulfilled*, *Cancelled*, or back to *Pending*).
- **Receive Daily Reminders:** Automated morning alerts remind staff of pending demands.

## D. Every Field, Button, Tab and Option

| Element | Type | What It Does |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Status Tabs** | Filter Buttons | Filters demands by *Pending*, *Ordered*, *Fulfilled*, *Cancelled*, or *All*. |
| **Mark Ordered / Fulfilled / Cancelled** | Row Buttons | Moves a demand through its lifecycle as you order and receive stock. |
| **Optional Note** | Text | Store customer phone number so you can call/WhatsApp when the medicine arrives. |

---

## E. Step-by-Step Instructions

1. When a patient asks for an out-of-stock medicine (e.g., *Insulatard Penfill*), click **New Product Demand** (`/user-demands/create`).
2. Select the medicine (or type its name), enter quantity (`2`), and add the patient's phone number in **Note**.
3. Click **Save**.
4. When your purchase manager places the distributor order, mark the row **Ordered**. When stock arrives and is handed to the patient, mark it **Fulfilled**.

---

## F. Practical Business Examples & Simple Calculations

### Example: Recovering Lost Sales
If 5 customers ask for *Humalog Mix 25* in one week (`Rs. 4,500` each) and staff log it in **User Demands**, the purchase manager orders 10 pens—turning `Rs. 22,500` of missed requests into completed sales.

## H. Common Mistakes and Solutions

| Mistake | Why It Happens | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Demand disappeared from list** | Default filter is **Pending**; once marked Ordered/Fulfilled, it moves to that tab. | Click **All** or the **Ordered / Fulfilled** status tab. |
