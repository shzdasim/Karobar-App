# How-To Guide 1: Adding a New Medicine (Pack vs. Unit & Pricing)

**Who is this for?** Store Owners, Inventory Managers, and Data-Entry Staff.  
**Full Module Reference:** [Products & Pricing](../modules/products.md)

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. Open **Items & Masters → Products → Add Product** (`/products/create`).
2. Enter the medicine **Name** (e.g., `Augmentin 625mg Tablet`).
3. Scan the **Barcode** on the box (or enter a store code).
4. Select **Category** (`Tablets`), **Brand** (`GSK`), and **Formulation (Generic / Salt)** (`Co-Amoxiclav`).
5. Set **Pack Size** to the number of sellable strips or tablets in one box (e.g., `6` strips per box).
6. Enter **Pack Purchase Price** (`Rs. 1,500`) and **Pack Sale Price** (`Rs. 1,800`). Karobar-App automatically calculates **Unit Purchase Cost** (`Rs. 250`) and **Unit Sale MRP** (`Rs. 300`).
7. Set **Rack / Shelf Code** (e.g., `R-04`) and **Drug Schedule** (`Schedule H`).
8. Click **Save**.

## Expected Result
- The medicine is ready for Purchase Invoices and Barcode POS, and cashiers can sell either full boxes or individual strips.
