# How-To Guide 6: Checking Stock, Batches & Running a Stocktake

**Who is this for?** Inventory Managers, Pharmacists, and Owners.  
**Full Module Reference:** [Batch & Expiry](../modules/batch-expiry.md) | [Stocktake Sessions](../modules/stocktake-sessions.md)

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### A. Quick Stock & Batch Check
1. Search any medicine in **Products** (`/products`) for total on-hand stock.
2. Open **Product Batches** (`/product-batches`) or **Batch & Expiry** (`/batch-expiry`) to inspect exact batch quantities, expiry dates, and shelf locations.

### B. Running a Shelf Stocktake
1. Go to **Stocktake Sessions → New Stocktake Session** (`/stocktake-sessions/create`).
2. Choose **Full Count** or **Cycle Count** (by Category, Brand, Supplier, or Location), keep **Blind Count Mode** ON, and click **Start Session**.
3. Count physical items on the shelf and enter **Counted Qty** per batch.
4. Manager opens **Review** and clicks **Finalize** to post verified variances automatically.
