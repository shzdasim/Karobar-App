# How-To Guide 10: Checking Daily/Monthly Business Profit & Expenses

**Who is this for?** Pharmacy Owners and Accountants.  
**Full Module Reference:** [Financial & Profit Reports](../modules/reports-financial.md) | [Operating Expenses](../modules/expenses.md)

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. Make sure all shop bills (rent, electricity, salaries, tea/petty cash) are recorded in **Operating Expenses** (`/expenses`).
2. Open **Reports → Net Profit (P&L)** (`/reports/net-profit`).
3. Choose **From Date** and **To Date** (for example, `1st` to `30th` of the month).
4. Review **Net Sales**, **Cost of Goods Sold**, **Gross Profit**, **Operating Expenses**, and **Net Profit**.
5. Click **Export PDF** or **Print P&L** for your monthly owner review.
