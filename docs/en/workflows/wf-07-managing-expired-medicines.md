# How-To Guide 7: Managing Near-Expiry & Expired Medicines (Quarantine)

**Who is this for?** Pharmacists, Inventory Managers, and Owners.  
**Full Module Reference:** [Batch & Expiry](../modules/batch-expiry.md) | [Quarantine & Disposal](../modules/quarantine-disposal.md)

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. Open **Stock & Inventory → Batch & Expiry** (`/batch-expiry`).
2. Click **Critical** or **Warning** (or filter by `3` months) to spot near-expiry batches.
3. For batches still accepted by your distributor, create a **Purchase Return** (`/purchase-returns/create`) immediately.
4. For already-expired or damaged batches, open **Quarantine & Disposal → New Disposal** (`/stock-disposals/create`), select the batch, and move it to **Quarantine** so cashiers cannot sell it.
5. When destroying quarantined stock, click **Dispose** (enter **Witness Name** for narcotics) and print the **Disposal Certificate**.
