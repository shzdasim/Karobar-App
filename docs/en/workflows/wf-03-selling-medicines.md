# How-To Guide 3: Selling Medicines at Barcode POS & Retail Counter

**Who is this for?** Counter Cashiers and Salesmen.  
**Full Module Reference:** [Barcode POS](../modules/barcode-pos.md) | [Sale Invoices](../modules/sale-invoices.md)

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. Make sure your **Cash Drawer** shift is open (`/cash-drawer`).
2. Open **Invoices → Barcode POS** (`/sale-invoices/pos`).
3. Scan the medicine barcode (`F2`) or press `F3` to search by medicine name or generic salt.
4. Confirm the auto-selected **FEFO Batch** and adjust **Pack / Unit** quantity.
5. If a patient is waiting for cash, press `F8` to **Hold Cart** and serve the next customer.
6. Select **Cash**, **Card**, **Mobile Wallet**, or **Split / Credit**, enter **Cash Received** (`Ctrl+C` for exact cash), and verify **Change Due**.
7. Press `F9` (or click **Complete Sale**) to save and print the thermal receipt.
