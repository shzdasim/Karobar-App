# How-To Guide 12: Generating & Printing Business Reports

**Who is this for?** Owners, Managers, Accountants, and Auditors.  
**Full Module Reference:** [Financial Reports](../modules/reports-financial.md) | [Sales & Purchase Reports](../modules/reports-sales-purchases.md) | [Inventory Reports](../modules/reports-inventory.md)

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. Open **Reports** in the left sidebar and pick the report you need (e.g., *Net Profit*, *Sale Detail*, *Current Stock*, *Near Expiry*, or *Narcotics*).
2. Set **From Date** and **To Date** (and any Brand, Supplier, Customer, or Category filter).
3. Click **Export CSV** for Excel analysis, **Export PDF** to save a formatted document, or **Print** for A4 paper printout.
