# How-To Guide 9: Collecting Customer Payments (Udhar) & Viewing Khata

**Who is this for?** Cashiers, Accountants, and Owners.  
**Full Module Reference:** [Customer Ledger](../modules/customer-ledger.md) | [Payment Vouchers](../modules/payments.md)

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. Open **Finance & Ledgers → Customer Ledger** (`/customer-ledger`) and select the customer to see their current receivable balance.
2. Click **Record Payment** (or go to `/payments` → **Record Payment** → **Customer Receipt**).
3. Enter the **Amount Received** (e.g., `Rs. 10,000`), select **Cash** (adds to active Cash Drawer) or **Bank / Wallet**, and allocate to pending credit invoices.
4. Click **Save**, then click **Print** or **Share** to send the updated Khata receipt on WhatsApp.
