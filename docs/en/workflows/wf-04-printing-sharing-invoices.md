# How-To Guide 4: Printing Invoices & Sharing on WhatsApp

**Who is this for?** Cashiers, Managers, and Accountants.  
**Full Module Reference:** [Printing & Sharing](../modules/printing-sharing.md)

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### A. Printing a Thermal or A4 Invoice
1. At POS, pressing `F9` / **Complete Sale** prints automatically if auto-print is enabled.
2. To reprint any past bill, open **Invoices → Sale Invoices** (`/sale-invoices`), find the bill, and click the **Print** icon.

### B. Sharing an Invoice or Ledger PDF on WhatsApp
1. Ensure WhatsApp is paired once under **Settings → Document Sharing → WhatsApp** (QR scan).
2. Open any saved **Sale Invoice**, **Purchase Order**, or **Customer Ledger** (`/customer-ledger`).
3. Click **Share**, confirm the recipient's phone number starts with `92` (e.g., `923001234567`), and click **Send via WhatsApp**.
