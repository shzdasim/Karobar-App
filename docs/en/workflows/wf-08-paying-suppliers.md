# How-To Guide 8: Paying Suppliers & Recording Supplier Credits

**Who is this for?** Accountants, Purchase Managers, and Owners.  
**Full Module Reference:** [Payment Vouchers](../modules/payments.md) | [Supplier Ledger](../modules/supplier-ledger.md)

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. Open **Finance & Ledgers → Payment Vouchers** (`/payments`) and click **Record Payment**.
2. Select **Supplier Payment** and choose the **Supplier** (e.g., *GSK Distributor*).
3. Enter the **Amount** paid (e.g., `Rs. 25,000`), select **Method** (*Cash*, *Bank*, *Mobile Wallet*, or *Cheque*), and choose your **Financial Account**.
4. Allocate the payment across their oldest unpaid Purchase Invoices (or leave unallocated as a **Party Credit** to apply later in `/party-credits`).
5. Click **Save** and print or WhatsApp the payment voucher.
