# How-To Guide 2: Creating a Purchase Invoice (Batches, Expiry & Bonus)

**Who is this for?** Purchase Staff, Inventory Managers, and Owners.  
**Full Module Reference:** [Purchase Invoices](../modules/purchase-invoices.md)

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. Open **Invoices → Purchase Invoices → Create** (`/purchase-invoices/create`).
2. Select the **Supplier** (e.g., *Ferozsons Distributor*) and enter the paper **Invoice #** and **Bill Amt**.
3. Search and add each medicine from the paper bill.
4. For each row, enter **Batch #**, **Expiry Date**, **Pack Qty**, **PBonus** (free bonus packs), **Pack Purchase Price**, and **Discount %**.
5. Confirm **Grand Total** matches the supplier's **Bill Amt**.
6. If paying cash/bank today, select **Payment Account** and enter **Amount Paid**; for credit bills, set **Due Date**.
7. Click **Save**.

## Example Calculation
`10` packs × `Rs. 250` (`Rs. 2,500`) minus `10%` discount (`Rs. 250`) = **`Rs. 2,250` payable**, while **`11` packs** (`10 + 1 bonus`) enter shelf stock.
