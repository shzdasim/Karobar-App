# How-To Guide 11: Finding Outstanding Customer & Supplier Balances

**Who is this for?** Owners, Accountants, and Managers.  
**Full Module Reference:** [Customer Ledger](../modules/customer-ledger.md) | [Supplier Ledger](../modules/supplier-ledger.md) | [Financial Reports](../modules/reports-financial.md)

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Quick Totals:** Open **Dashboard → Financials & Credit** tab to see total Customer Receivables and Supplier Payables.
2. **Aging Breakdown (Who Owes Longest):** Open **Reports → Outstanding Aging** (`/reports/outstanding-aging`) to view balances grouped by `0–30`, `31–60`, `61–90`, and `90+` days overdue.
3. **Single Party Statement:** Open **Customer Ledger** (`/customer-ledger`) or **Supplier Ledger** (`/supplier-ledger`) and select the party to see every invoice, return, and payment.
