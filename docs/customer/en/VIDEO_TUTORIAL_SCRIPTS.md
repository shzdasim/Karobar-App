# Video Tutorial Scripts — Major Modules (English)

Ready-to-record 2–3 minute screen-recording scripts for onboarding Pakistani pharmacy teams.

---

## Video 1: First-Time Setup & Dashboard Tour (2:30)
- **Hook:** "Welcome to Karobar-App—your complete pharmacy and medical store ERP."
- **Visuals:** Login (`/`) → Setup Wizard (`/setup`) → Dashboard (`/dashboard`).
- **Narration:** Show entering Pharmacy Name, Drug License, PKR currency, and 80mm thermal printer. Walk through Dashboard KPI cards (Today's Sales, Net Profit, Receivables, Payables) and Near-Expiry alerts.

## Video 2: Adding Medicines, Pack Size & Batches (3:00)
- **Hook:** "Stop losing track of strips vs. full boxes."
- **Visuals:** `/products/create` → `/purchase-invoices/create`.
- **Narration:** Create *Augmentin 625mg* with `Pack Size = 6`. Explain Purchase Price vs. Sale Price/MRP and Minimum Stock Alert. Then enter a distributor Purchase Invoice with Batch No, Expiry Date, and Bonus quantity.

## Video 3: Fast Counter Billing with Barcode POS (2:30)
- **Hook:** "Bill a customer in under 10 seconds."
- **Visuals:** `/cash-drawer` (Open Shift) → `/sale-invoices/pos`.
- **Narration:** Open morning shift (`Rs. 5,000`). Scan *Panadol*, show automatic FEFO batch selection, sell `2` strips, hold a bill for a waiting customer, resume it, enter cash received, and print thermal receipt.

## Video 4: Sale Returns & Supplier Expiry Returns (2:30)
- **Hook:** "Handle returns cleanly without messing up your stock or Khata."
- **Visuals:** `/sale-returns/create` → `/purchase-returns/create`.
- **Narration:** Search original Sale Invoice, return `1` unopened syrup bottle, and refund cash. Next, open `/batch-expiry`, spot a near-expiry batch, and create a Purchase Return to the distributor.

## Video 5: Customer & Supplier Khata, Payments & Expenses (2:30)
- **Hook:** "Know every rupee owed to you and by you."
- **Visuals:** `/customer-ledger` → `/payments` → `/expenses`.
- **Narration:** Open a regular customer's ledger, record a `Rs. 10,000` partial recovery in Payment Vouchers, allocate it to pending invoices, and share the PDF receipt on WhatsApp. Record a daily shop expense.

## Video 6: Stocktake, Quarantine & Nightly Closing (3:00)
- **Hook:** "Lock down your inventory and close your cash drawer with zero surprises."
- **Visuals:** `/stocktake-sessions` → `/stock-disposals/create` → `/cash-drawer` (Close Shift) → `/reports/net-profit`.
- **Narration:** Quarantine an expired batch, run a quick shelf stocktake, close the Cash Drawer shift by counting physical cash, and check today's Net Profit report and automatic backup.
