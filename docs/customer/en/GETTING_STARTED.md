# Getting Started with Karobar-App (Pharmacy & Medical Store Guide)

Welcome to **Karobar-App** by **SofTech Web Solutions**. This guide helps pharmacy owners, counter cashiers, and store managers start using Karobar-App in minutes—without technical jargon.

---

## 1. First-Time Login & Setup Wizard

1. **Open Karobar-App** on your Windows PC (or open the browser URL if connecting from a LAN counter terminal).
2. **Sign In:** Enter your store email/username and password on the Login screen (`/`).
3. **Activate License (if prompted):** On first launch, enter your license key on the Activation screen (`/activate`). Your license is bound to your store PC.
4. **Complete the Setup Wizard (`/setup`):**
   - Enter your **Pharmacy / Medical Store Name**, **Address**, **Phone Number**, and **DRAP / Drug License Number** (printed on receipts).
   - Choose your **Currency** (default: `PKR / Rs.`) and **Sale System Mode** (*Retail Only* or *Retail + Wholesale*).
   - Configure your **Thermal Printer** (80mm or 58mm) or **A4 Printer**.

## 2. Understanding the Main Screen

![Dashboard](../../../Screenshots/Dashboard.webp)

- **Left Sidebar:** Organized into 7 clear sections:
  1. **Items & Masters** (Products, Product Batches, Categories, Brands, Suppliers, Customers, Doctors, Barcode Studio)
  2. **Invoices** (Barcode POS, Sale Invoices, Wholesale Invoices, Trade Schemes, Purchase Invoices, Purchase Orders)
  3. **Returns** (Sale Returns, Purchase Returns)
  4. **Stock & Inventory** (Batch & Expiry, User Demands, Stock Adjustments, Stocktake Sessions, Quarantine & Disposal)
  5. **Finance & Ledgers** (Customer Ledger, Supplier Ledger, Payment Vouchers, Party Credits, Cash Drawer, Financial Accounts, Operating Expenses, Double-Entry & GL*, HR & Payroll*)
  6. **Reports** (Net Profit, Cost of Sale, Sale/Purchase Detail, Current Stock, Near Expiry, Narcotics, etc.)
  7. **System & Settings** (Settings, Users, Roles, Backups & Storage)
  *(Note: Modules marked with `*` depend on your active license tier.)*
- **Starred Favorites:** Click the star icon next to any menu item (e.g., *Barcode POS*, *Batch & Expiry*) to pin it at the top of the sidebar.
- **Topbar:** Quick access to **Cash Drawer status**, **Notifications**, **Theme Toggle**, **Keyboard Shortcuts**, and **Cube AI Assistant** (English & Urdu help).

## 3. Your First 5 Steps in the Store

1. **Add Categories & Brands:** Go to **Categories** (`/categories`) and **Brands** (`/brands`)—for example, *Tablets*, *Syrups*, *Injections* and *GSK*, *Abbott*, *Getz Pharma*. (You can also bulk-import CSV files.)
2. **Add Your First Medicine:** Go to **Products → Add Product** (`/products/create`), enter *Panadol 500mg*, set **Pack Size** (`10` strips or `100` tablets), **Purchase Price**, **Sale Price**, and **Minimum Stock Alert**.
3. **Add Your Supplier & Opening Purchase:** Add your distributor under **Suppliers** (`/suppliers/create`), then create a **Purchase Invoice** (`/purchase-invoices/create`) with **Batch Number** and **Expiry Date** so stock enters your shelf.
4. **Open Your Cash Drawer Shift:** Go to **Cash Drawer** (`/cash-drawer`) and click **Open Shift** with your morning cash float (e.g., `Rs. 5,000`).
5. **Make Your First Sale:** Open **Barcode POS** (`/sale-invoices/pos`) or **Sale Invoices → Create Retail** (`/sale-invoices/create/retail`), scan or search *Panadol*, press **Save & Print**, and hand the thermal receipt to the customer.
